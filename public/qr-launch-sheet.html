Rabbit Superuser Management QR Sheet

Testing-only QR sheet. These codes target GitHub-hosted resources from the published repo. They do not contain root payloads, secrets, or privileged commands.

Release QR is blocked until the Rabbit can reach a broker endpoint over HTTPS or through a validated Rabbit-native route. If setup passes but route fails, stop there.

Safe first run

  1. Open the PWA QR first and confirm the UI loads.
  2. Run setup. If broker is unreachable, stop testing later buttons.
  3. Use the Creation manifest QR only for the single management Creation.
  4. Use the lease pairing QR only for broker ownership metadata.
  5. Stop before ADB, fastboot, root, reboot modes, or device changes unless separately authorized live.
